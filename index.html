03 / A more useful shopping process

Turn browsing
into a shortlist.

A Kakobuy shopping guide is most useful when it helps you ask better questions. Save the details that matter to your purchase, not just the product image.

Start researching on CAFANS ↗

  1. Choose the role in your wardrobe.

    Decide whether you need an everyday sneaker, a warm layer or a finishing accessory. Begin with one category.

  2. Record comparable information.

    Save the listing URL, selected variant, measurements, stated material and the date you checked the price.

  3. Inspect the evidence.

    Compare available close-ups and seller information. Quality-control photos can show visible details but do not prove authenticity.

  4. Check the complete cost.

    Consider delivery, service charges and applicable taxes. Recheck availability and return conditions before ordering.

05 / Straight answers

Before you
follow a product link.

A few practical answers for anyone using a Kakobuy spreadsheet as a starting point for shopping research.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is generally a curated list of product links used to organize shopping research. Lists may include categories, prices, seller details or notes, but their accuracy and freshness depend on whoever maintains them.

Is this an official Kakobuy website or a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent browsing guide that links to CAFANS categories. It is not operated by Kakobuy and does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet or live inventory feed.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Compare the specific listing’s measurements with an item you already own. For clothing, check chest width, length, shoulders and waist where relevant. For shoes, check the seller’s size chart and foot-length guidance instead of assuming labels are identical across brands.

Do product photos prove authenticity or quality?

No. Photos can help you inspect visible details, but they do not establish authenticity, durability or seller reliability. Verify product provenance and seller information independently, especially for branded items.

What should I include in my shopping budget?

Include the item price, domestic delivery, any service fees, international shipping and applicable taxes or duties. Availability and charges vary, so confirm current figures before ordering.

Where do the links on this page go?

General browsing links open www.cafans.com. Category links open the corresponding category on cafans.com. Check the destination listing for current product details and availability.
